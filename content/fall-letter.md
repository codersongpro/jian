# A Letter to Fall

사진 본문 전사. 이름·학급·학원 표시는 제외했다. 줄바꿈으로 나뉜 단어는 이어 적었으며 문법·철자는 교정하지 않았다. 원본 학습지 이미지와 도안은 공개용에서 제외했다.

Dear Fall,

Fall, it was very fun and joyous with you.
You gave me present like fruits, vegetable, and acorns.
It is very precious to me because you gave it to me.
Also, I went to the camping because its fall.
My dad said, "When the fall comes, animals invite each other and have a wonderful day."
I had wonderful day with you at the past days.
Fall, I will going to miss you!
Have a good and joyous day despite you will have to go now.
I love you Fall!
